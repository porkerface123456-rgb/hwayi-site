FAQ

How do I book an airport transfer?

Use the online booking form, or add us on LINE / WeChat (ID: kiki939779). We will confirm by LINE or phone.

What information do you need?

Date and time, flight number, pickup and drop-off addresses, number of passengers and pieces of luggage. A flight number is required for airport pickups.

Is the price per person or per vehicle?

Per vehicle. The price is the same however many people ride, with no per-person charge.

What if my flight is delayed?

We track your actual arrival time by flight number and the driver adjusts. If your flight is delayed, please stay in touch with the driver after landing.

Where will the driver wait at the airport?

The driver waits in the airport car park. After landing, please contact the driver to agree on a meeting exit.

I only use LINE. Do I need to give a phone number?

No. Either a phone number or a LINE ID is enough, and international numbers are fine.
